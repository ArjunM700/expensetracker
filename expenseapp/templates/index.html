About ExpenseTracker

Managing personal finances shouldn't require complex spreadsheets or a degree in accounting. We built ExpenseTracker to solve a simple problem: knowing exactly where your money goes without the headaches.

Our platform strips away the unnecessary clutter found in modern financial apps. We focus purely on providing a fast, secure, and straightforward way to log your daily incomes and expenses.

By giving you immediate access to daily, weekly, and monthly aggregates—alongside detailed filtering and historical reporting—we empower you to make informed decisions and hit your savings goals on your own terms.

Secure & Private

Your financial data belongs to you. We've built our platform on a foundation of privacy and security.

Encrypted Sessions

Data remains secured in transit and at rest with modern encryption standards, ensuring your financial logs are visible only to you.

Zero Data Selling

We do not sell, share, or cross-target your personal budget records with advertising networks. You are not the product.
